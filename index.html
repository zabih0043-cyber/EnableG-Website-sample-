Three questions

Most people arrive with one of these.

You do not have to work through them in order, and nothing is locked. Start with whichever one is actually keeping you up.

5 min

"Where am I, really?"

The Life Balance Wheel asks you to rate ten areas — work, family, health, finance, growth and the rest — from one to ten. You see which area is starving while the others eat.

4 min

"Why can't I get moving?"

Twenty questions across six areas: how you think, your energy, your habits, what you feel, your direction, and the people around you. You get a score out of 100 and three things to try.

10 min

"Will this month work out?"

Set out what the month costs, what is coming in, and the gap between them. Then it looks at your skills and suggests ways to close it — ending with what to do next, not just a number.

No one is watching you use these

You can be honest in them, because nothing you type is sent anywhere or seen by anyone.
