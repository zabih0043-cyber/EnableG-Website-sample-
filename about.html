About Enable G

Most advice assumes you have time.

Time, money, and a clear head. Enable G is built for when you have none of those — seven small tools, 4 to 10 minutes each, asking plain questions and giving you one thing to do about the answer. Free, because a tool you cannot afford is not a tool.

What we believe

One thing you actually do beats ten you agree with.

Every tool here ends with three suggestions, and we would rather you did one of them than read all three and closed the tab. That is why nothing asks for an email address first, and why none of them take longer than ten minutes.

The questions are deliberately blunt. You cannot get a useful answer out of a tool you were not honest with, and it is easier to be honest when nobody is watching — so nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What it costs

Nothing. No account, no email address, no trial that ends.

Being straight with you

What these tools are not.

Worth saying plainly, because a tool that oversells itself is worse than no tool. If what you are carrying is bigger than this, these will not be enough, and that is not a failure on your part.

Not therapy

The questions about how you feel can show you a pattern. They cannot treat anything. If the weight has been heavy for a while, please talk to a doctor or a counsellor.

Not financial advice

The money tools do arithmetic on numbers you enter and suggest where to look. They do not know your situation, and nobody here is a licensed adviser.

Not a substitute for the hard part

A score is not a plan, and finishing an assessment is not the same as changing anything. The tools are there to tell you where to point the effort.

Growth support for people seeking more clarity, stability, and beauty in the way they move forward.
